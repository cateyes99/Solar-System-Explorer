## Pluto Map

pluto.jpg: NASA Photojournal PIA19858, "Global Map of Pluto", released July 27, 2015.
Source: [NASA New Horizons global map](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/global-map-of-pluto/).
Credit: NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute.
Downloaded as an unannotated 2048-pixel-wide derivative from NASA's image service:
https://assets.science.nasa.gov/dynamicimage/assets/science/psd/photojournal/pia/pia19/pia19858/PIA19858.jpg?w=2048&fit=clip

This cylindrical grayscale mosaic combines observations from July 7-14, 2015, with resolution ranging from about 40 km/pixel to 400 m/pixel. The central anti-Charon hemisphere is most detailed. Unobserved areas and resolution differences are retained; this is not a complete true-color surface or an elevation map. Longitudes are aligned with a half-width texture offset for Three.js's sphere UV convention.

All other planet, cloud, corona, and star textures are generated by this application.
