# Earth Map

earth.jpg: NASA Blue Marble imagery, distributed in the Three.js examples.
Source: [Three.js Earth map](https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg).
NASA imagery is generally not subject to copyright in the United States.
[NASA media guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/).

All other planet, cloud, corona, and star textures are generated by this application.
